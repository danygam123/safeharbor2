Immediate Overdose Response Steps

1 Shout and Rub: Try to wake them up. Rub knuckles hard on their sternum (breastbone).
2 Call 911: If they are unresponsive and have slow/no breathing, dial 911 immediately.
3 Naloxone: Administer Narcan nasal spray if available. Repeat after 2-3 mins if needed.
4 Recovery Position: Turn them onto their side so they don't choke. Stay with them.
