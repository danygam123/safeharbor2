Immediate Overdose Response Steps

1 Shout and Rub: Try to wake them up. Rub knuckles hard on their sternum (breastbone).
2 Call 911: If unresponsive with slow/no breathing, dial 911 immediately.
3 Naloxone: Administer Narcan nasal spray if available. Repeat after 2–3 mins if needed.
4 Recovery Position: Turn them onto their side so they don't choke. Stay with them.

Privacy & Data

What We Collect

SafeHarbor collects only what is necessary for personal wellness features:

  • Email address — required to create an account and log in.
  • Daily check-in responses — mood, stress, optional notes. Private to your account.
  • Journal entries — text you write. Private to your account.
  • Personal goals — text you set. Private to your account.
  • Learning activity — which resources you've explored (stored locally on your device).

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, school, or any information not listed above.

Where Data Is Stored

Account data is stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database provider. Resource exploration and bookmarks are stored locally on your device in browser storage and are not sent to any server.

AI Companion

When you use the AI Companion, your chat messages are sent to Google Gemini to generate responses. Your journal entries and check-in responses are never automatically sent to the AI — only the messages you type into the chat are shared. Chat history is not stored in our database.

Who Can See Your Data

Only you. Row Level Security (RLS) is enabled in our database, meaning database queries are enforced server-side so that no other user — including administrators — can read your check-ins or journal entries.

How to Delete Your Data

Go to My Account → Delete My Account & Data. This removes all your stored check-ins, journal entries, and goals. To complete full account deletion, contact us at privacy@safeharbor.app.

Additional Notes

SafeHarbor is an educational tool. We do not sell, share, or monetize personal data. We do not use third-party analytics. If you are a minor, please review this page with a trusted adult.
